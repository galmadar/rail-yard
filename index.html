Working the yard

← / → — drive left, right across the screen
W / S — drive nose first, back
Space — brake
1 2 3 4 5 — swing the points
Q / E — slide the pin left, right
U — pull the pin, cut off
F — ride along with the shunter, or park again
C — put the camera back where it started
N — next job
R — start this job again

drag — turn the view around
ctrl or ⌘ + drag — slide the camera across the yard
wheel — zoom in and out
